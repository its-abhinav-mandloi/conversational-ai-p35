## Section 1: Water Management and Alternate Wetting and Drying
While lowland rice tolerates flooded conditions, continuous deep flooding is not always required throughout the entire vegetative phase. Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) is a water-saving educational practice in which shallow ponded water is allowed to subside until the perched water table drops slightly below the soil surface before re-irrigating. Maintaining shallow standing moisture during active panicle initiation and flowering helps minimize spikelet sterility caused by moisture stress.

## Section 2: Nursery Raising and Transplanting Fundamentals
Healthy rice seedlings support uniform stand establishment. Educational nursery guides emphasize well-leveled seedbeds, balanced organic matter, and careful pulling of seedlings at roughly 20 to 25 days (for medium-duration varieties) to reduce root shock. Transplanting at consistent shallow depth encourages earlier tillering compared with deep planting.

## Section 3: Yellowing Leaves and Mid-Season Nitrogen Diagnostics
Uniform pale green or yellowing older leaves in transplanted rice often reflect limited nitrogen availability or root stress from stagnant anaerobic soil conditions. Educational IPM programs use visual reference tools such as standardized Leaf Color Charts (LCC) to observe relative greenness across representative field hills rather than relying on calendar-based assumptions alone. Any corrective nutrient plan should be aligned with local soil test guidance.

## Section 4: Integrated Pest Scouting for Rice Blast and Stem Borers
Rice blast presents as spindle-shaped lesions with gray centers and brown margins on leaves or at the panicle neck, favored by high humidity and dense canopies. Stem borers cause "deadhearts" in vegetative tillers and "whiteheads" after panicle emergence. Non-prescriptive IPM practices prioritize resistant cultivars, balanced canopy spacing for airflow, conserving natural predators like spiders and mirid bugs, and contacting regional plant clinics if economic thresholds are approached.
